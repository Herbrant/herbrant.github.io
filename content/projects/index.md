## ExamBox

ExamBox is a software project designed to support laboratory exams at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science of the University of Catania. It enables instructors to generate minimal GNU/Linux images with all the tools required for an exam and deploy them across departmental laboratories in a diskless environment using iPXE. The generated images include security mechanisms that help prevent student collaboration and access to unauthorized materials during examinations.

Davide Carnemolla, Mario Di Raimondo

## DropTheMark

DropTheMark is a web application for publishing exam results while preserving student privacy. It is currently used at the Department of Mathematics and Computer Science of the University of Catania.

Davide Carnemolla, Mario Di Raimondo
